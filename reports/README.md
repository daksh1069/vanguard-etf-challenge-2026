# reports/ — the trading-phase write-ups

Everything here belongs to the trading phase of the challenge (14 Sep – 6 Nov 2026). The finals pitch lives in
`docs/pitch/` and `presentation/`.

**Start with `WOLFPACK_STRATEGY.md`.** It is the final, consolidated strategy and decision record (17 Sep 2026); the
other documents are supporting detail and nothing in them overrides it.

## Strategy documents

| file | what it is |
|---|---|
| **WOLFPACK_STRATEGY.md** | The final strategy: the order, why it works under a 0% risk-free Sharpe, Book A vs Book B, distribution crediting, rebalancing, the field, dates, and the errors we caught. |
| **DECISION_RECORD.md** | The evidence chain behind Book A: the regime catch on Book B's 1.358 figure, all five backtests, the corrected rebalancing analysis, and a log of every error fixed. |
| **STRATEGY.md** | Book A on its own terms: mechanism, methodology, competitor scorecard, why a high-risk sleeve fails, and an appendix on the field's (anonymised) trade notes. |
| **ORDER_TICKET.md** | The six orders for the opening trade (17 Sep). |
| **WHY_TRADE_NOW.md** | One-pager for the team: why we placed the orders immediately rather than waiting. |
