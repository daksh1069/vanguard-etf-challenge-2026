About 50 seconds. Liquidity and scale. On the bond side, the index is an 823-billion-dollar market of developed-market government bonds, so a 10-billion-dollar fund would be about 1.2% of it. On the hedge side, 81% of the currency is in the five most traded major currencies. In March 2020, forward spreads widened three to six times in our Bloomberg data, but they stayed small in absolute terms. And Vanguard already runs this machinery: BNDX hedges 124 billion dollars, and its ETF trades at a 0.021% median bid-ask spread. In stress, two things need planning. Forward losses settle in cash when the dollar falls fast, so the fund holds a cash buffer. And roll costs widen, so hedges roll through FX swaps.
