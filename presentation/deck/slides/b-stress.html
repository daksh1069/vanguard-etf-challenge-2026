Backup. Use if asked about behaviour in calm versus stressed markets. Calm years, 2016 to 2019: 2.2% a year against 1.5% for VGSH. BNDX did better then, 4.4%, because falling yields reward long duration, and that same duration cost it 12.8% in 2022. March 2020: flat, while Treasuries rallied as the safe haven. 2022: the smallest loss of the bond funds. Since 2023 it has matched VGSH at 3.9% a year. Bills are ahead at 4.7% because short rates have been high. Volatility over the whole period is about the same as VGSH. Drawdowns are on month-end values, so intra-month falls were deeper.
