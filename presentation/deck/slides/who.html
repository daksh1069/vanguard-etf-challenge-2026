About 45 seconds. Who is it for? Three groups. First, investors who already hold US short bonds like VGSH or BSV, and want a second set of central banks behind them, without currency swings. Second, advisers. Vanguard’s own research suggests about 30% of a bond allocation in hedged international bonds, but today Vanguard’s only US option for that is BNDX, at 6.4 years. Third, people in ISHG or BWZ who want short foreign bonds without 7% currency volatility and a 0.35% fee. One honest note: Treasury interest is exempt from state tax and this fund’s isn’t, so it fits best in IRAs, 401(k)s and tax-exempt accounts.
