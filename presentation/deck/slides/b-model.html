The simulation got the return and 2022 right but understated risk by about half. That’s why every headline number in this deck uses real prices.

Simulation: team Model C, 2013–2026. Real prices: scripts/real_hedged_full.py, 2013 or 2015 to Oct 2026

Backup. Use if asked how we validated our numbers, or about the simulation in our earlier work. We built a simulation first. When we rebuilt the fund from real prices, the return and the 2022 result matched, but the simulation’s volatility was about half the real figure, and its worst drawdown was too small. So we dropped it from the headline and kept it only as a cross-check. Ranges show different start dates (2013 or 2015) and whether Korea is included.
