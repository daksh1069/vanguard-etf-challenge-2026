About 40 seconds. The strongest evidence of demand is that Vanguard already sells this, just not here. Its Global Short-Term Bond Index Fund, hedged to dollars, is domiciled in Ireland. It holds 7.7 billion dollars, charges 0.10%, and has returned 2.1% a year over ten years. It’s not identical. It runs out to five years and holds US and corporate bonds, so it lost more in 2022 and tracks VGSH more closely. Ours is the cleaner version: government bonds only, one to three years, outside the US. And BNDX’s 124 billion dollars shows US investors want hedged international bonds.
