Backup. Use if asked what we assumed to build the series. The biggest one is A1: ISHG’s real returns stand in for the index. That’s conservative, because ISHG’s returns already include its trading costs, and we only swap its 0.35% fee for our 0.10%. A2: we hold today’s currency mix constant for the whole history. That only affects the hedge, and a mismatch would leave a little currency unhedged, which is probably why our volatility is slightly high. A3: the main case leaves out the Korean won because Korea only joined the index this year; including it adds 0.07% a year.
