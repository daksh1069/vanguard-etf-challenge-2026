About 55 seconds. Here’s the result. A hundred dollars in February 2015 grows to 123 in our rebuilt fund, 121 in VGSH and 122 in BNDX, though BNDX took a much bumpier road to get there. Over the ten calendar years 2016 to 2025, the fund returned 1.94% a year, against 1.78% for VGSH. Volatility was 1.7%, about the same as VGSH, so we’re not claiming it’s safer than Treasuries. The number we care most about is 0.69, the correlation with VGSH. Foreign central banks move on their own schedule, so this gives a short-bond investor a genuinely different source of return. And to be upfront: T-bills, the yellow line, ended at 128. This isn’t a cash replacement. It belongs in the bond allocation.
