About 50 seconds. We’d rather tell you the risks ourselves. First, there’s no track record. Our numbers are a rebuild from real prices, and the Irish fund is a cousin, not this fund. Second, it doesn’t beat T-bills: 1.9% against 2.2% a year over the decade. It’s a bond diversifier, not a cash product. Third, taxes. Taxable investors give up the state-tax exemption on Treasuries, roughly 0.2 to 0.4 points a year in high-tax states, which is why it suits retirement accounts. Fourth, one decade of data, mostly with US rates above foreign rates. Last, two launch items: confirming a hedged version of the FTSE index, and deciding whether to cap or leave out Korea, which joined the index this year.
