About 50 seconds. So here’s the product. It tracks the FTSE World Government Bond Index, developed markets, 1 to 3 years, excluding the US, in its dollar-hedged version. That’s about 190 government bonds, an 823-billion-dollar market, with an average duration of about 1.9 years. Every country is capped at 21%, so no single government dominates. The euro is still just over half the currency exposure, because it covers several countries, followed by the yen and sterling. All of that currency is hedged back to dollars every month. We propose a 0.10% fee, the same as Vanguard’s equivalent fund in Europe. And capacity isn’t a concern: a 10-billion-dollar fund would own about 1.2% of the index.
