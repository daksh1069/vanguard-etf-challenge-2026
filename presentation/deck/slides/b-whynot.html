“Why not just buy…?”

QuestionAnswer
Why not just VGSH?About the same return and risk, but a different set of central banks: correlation about 0.7.
Why not BNDX?6.4 years’ duration cost 12.8% in 2022. We keep the hedge at 1.9 years (−2.8%).
Why not ISHG or BWZ?Unhedged: −11% in 2022 and 7% volatility, mostly currency, plus a 0.35% fee.
Why not T-bills?Bills won 2016–2025 (2.2% vs 1.9% a year). This belongs in the bond allocation, not cash.
Why not the Irish fund?It’s an Irish fund built for investors outside the US, runs to 5 years and holds US and corporate bonds.
What about Korea?Joined the index April–November 2026. The won is hedged with non-deliverable forwards, so we can cap or exclude it.
Why Vanguard?It already runs the Irish fund ($7.7B, 0.10%) and BNDX ($124B). The hedging scale exists.
