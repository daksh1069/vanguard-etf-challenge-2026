About 55 seconds. The question that matters for any bond ETF is how it behaves in calm and in stressed markets. This decade gave us two stress tests. The 2022 rate shock: our fund lost 2.8%. VGSH lost 3.9%, BSV 5.5%, Vanguard’s Irish fund 5.1%, and BNDX 12.8%. Short duration did most of the work, and the foreign curves helped a little more. March 2020, the dash for cash, is the honest case. Money ran into US Treasuries, so VGSH rose 1.3%. Our fund was flat, minus 0.1%, while BNDX lost 2.9% and unhedged ISHG lost 1.4%. So it doesn’t replace Treasuries as a crisis hedge, but it held its value. Over the whole period, the worst fall from a peak was 4.9%, against 5.3% for VGSH and 14.9% for BNDX.
