About 35 seconds. Start with 2022, the year rates rose fastest. Say you were a US investor who wanted international bonds without currency swings. Vanguard’s answer is BNDX, and in 2022 it lost 12.8%. US short Treasuries, VGSH, lost 3.9%. The hedge didn’t cause BNDX’s loss. Its duration did: 6.4 years. So the obvious question is why a US investor can’t buy hedged foreign government bonds with short duration. Today, in the US-listed market, they can’t. That’s the gap.
