About 50 seconds. Now the evidence. A fund that doesn’t exist has no track record, and our early simulation turned out to understate risk by about half. So we rebuilt the fund from real market prices instead. We started with ISHG’s actual returns. ISHG holds exactly these bonds, unhedged. Then we hedged every currency: real one-month forwards from Bloomberg for the five major currencies, which are 81% of the fund, and interest-rate gaps for the seven smaller ones. We also moved the fee from ISHG’s 0.35% to our 0.10%. That gives a monthly series from February 2015 to October 2026, which we compare with real funds: VGSH, BSV, BNDX, T-bills and Vanguard’s Irish fund.
